Decision Trace
Reconstructing human–AI decision processes
An agent that converts a user's account of an AI interaction into a structured sequence of decisions.
About the Tool

AI-assisted work is often represented only through its final output. Decision Trace focuses instead on the actions that occur during the process. Describe one interaction with an AI-generated result, and the agent will identify moments of selection, modification, rejection, and confirmation.

Trace an Interaction

Describe what the AI generated, what you chose or changed, what you removed, and how the final result was accepted. The agent will reconstruct the sequence without inventing actions that were not described.

Decision Vocabulary

Select

The human chooses one option from multiple AI-generated possibilities.

Modify

The human retains an AI-generated result but changes part of it.

Reject

The human removes, refuses, ignores, or discards a result or part of it.

Confirm

The human reviews and accepts a result as final.

Project Role

Each conversation is stored in Firebase as a record of human–AI interaction. These records can later become source data for visualizing decision-making as a process: one decision event becomes one cell, and multiple traces form a larger tissue.
